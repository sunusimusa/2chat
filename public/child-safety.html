2Chat Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

2Chat is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

2Chat does not permit content or activity that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

2. Prohibited Child Sexual Abuse Material

2Chat strictly prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, sexualization of children, grooming, and other conduct that sexually harms or exploits minors.

Users must not upload, publish, request, distribute, promote, or otherwise use 2Chat to facilitate such material or activity.

3. Content and Account Enforcement

2Chat may remove, restrict, or disable access to content that violates our Terms, applicable law, safety requirements, or platform policies.

We may restrict, suspend, or terminate accounts when we reasonably believe that an account or its activity violates our Terms, applicable law, creates security risks, involves abuse, or harms other users or the 2Chat platform.

4. Reports and Safety Concerns

Safety concerns and reports concerning potentially harmful content or activity may be reviewed by 2Chat and handled according to applicable laws, safety requirements, and our platform policies.

Reports and valid legal requests concerning content may result in content removal, restriction, account enforcement, or other appropriate action.

5. Cooperation With Authorities

Where required by applicable law, 2Chat may cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement, regulatory, or child-protection authorities concerning child safety, child sexual exploitation, CSAM, or other serious safety matters.

Where legally required, reports may be made to relevant authorities and information may be disclosed in response to valid legal requests.

6. User Responsibility

Users must not use 2Chat to exploit, abuse, groom, threaten, or sexually exploit children.

Users must also comply with applicable laws and the 2Chat Terms of Service when using the platform.
