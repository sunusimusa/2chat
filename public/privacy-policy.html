2Chat Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2Chat collects, uses, stores, protects and shares information when you use the 2Chat app, website and related services. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Policy applies to information processed through 2Chat. The Service is a social communication platform with account profiles, posts, messaging, groups, status updates, short videos, notifications, calling and, where enabled, virtual coins, gifts and creator-related wallet features.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

When you register or manage your account, we may process your username, email address, phone number, date of birth, password hash, account type, profile photo/avatar, cover image, biography, creator status and account preferences. We do not need your plain-text password; passwords are stored in a hashed form.

2.2 Social Activity and User Content

When you use social features, we may process posts, text, uploaded images and videos, Shorts, captions, hashtags, comments, likes, views, shares, saved videos, status updates, friend connections, followers, following, blocked-user lists and reports.

2.3 Messages, Groups and Calls

Depending on the features you use, we may process message text, images, voice messages, supported files, replies, reactions, delivery and read status, message timestamps, group membership and group messages. Call features may process call signaling information, participant identifiers, call status and technical connection information needed to establish and maintain calls.

Do not assume that messages or calls use end-to-end encryption. This Policy does not promise that call media is never recorded by a participant's device or that recipients cannot save or copy content they receive.

2.4 Wallet, Coins, Gifts and Payments

If you use these features, we may process coin balances, purchases, spending, gifts sent and received, creator earnings, platform commissions, withdrawal balances, withdrawal requests and transaction records. Payment providers may separately collect payment details. 2Chat may receive transaction references, payment status and other information needed to confirm and reconcile a transaction.

2.5 Device, Log and Technical Information

When you access the Service, our systems and service providers may process IP address, device and browser information, operating system, app or web version, log and request data, timestamps, diagnostic information, crash or error details, security events and network information. This information helps operate, troubleshoot and protect the Service.

2.6 Push Notification Information

If notifications are enabled, we may process push-notification tokens and notification preferences so that your device can receive account, message and service notifications. You can control notification permissions through your device settings, although disabling them may affect timely alerts.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create, authenticate and manage accounts and profiles.
  • Provide posts, feeds, status updates, Shorts, messaging, groups, calls and social connections.
  • Store, deliver and display content you choose to upload or send.
  • Operate notifications, search, moderation, reporting, blocking and account-safety features.
  • Maintain wallet records, process coin purchases, gifts and eligible creator withdrawals.
  • Verify transactions, prevent fraud, investigate abuse and enforce our Terms.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the reliability of the Service.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal Grounds for Processing

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the Service you request, perform our agreement with you, comply with legal obligations, protect users and the Service, pursue legitimate operational and security interests, or rely on your consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to legal and technical limits.

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information as a business model. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: profile details and content you choose to make public, plus messages or files with the recipients or group you choose. Public posts, Shorts, comments and interactions may be visible beyond your immediate connections.
  • Service providers: infrastructure providers that support hosting, databases, media storage and delivery, payment processing, push notifications, email delivery and security. They receive information needed to provide services to 2Chat.
  • Payment and transaction partners: transaction information needed to process, confirm, reconcile or investigate purchases and eligible payouts.
  • Legal and safety purposes: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to respond to lawful requests, protect users, investigate fraud, address security incidents or protect legal rights.
  • Business changes: if 2Chat or its operation is involved in a merger, restructuring, financing or transfer, information may be handled as part of that process subject to applicable law.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

2Chat's current technical stack includes a hosted application/server, a MongoDB database, Cloudinary for media storage or delivery, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications and Paystack-related payment processing. The exact providers and processing locations may change as the Service develops. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy documentation and, where applicable, our instructions.

When you use third-party services or follow links outside 2Chat, their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

7. Public and Shared Content

Your username, avatar, bio, public posts, public videos, comments and public interactions may be visible to other users. Content sent in a direct or group conversation is made available to the recipients you select. Recipients may take screenshots, download, copy, forward or otherwise retain content. Deleting content from 2Chat cannot guarantee deletion of copies already saved by others or from their devices.

8. Storage, Security and International Transfers

Information may be stored in databases and infrastructure operated by 2Chat or its service providers. Depending on provider locations and network routing, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Service, such as access controls and protected password storage. No internet transmission, device or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You should protect your credentials and notify us if you suspect account compromise.

9. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain account and conversation functionality, preserve wallet and transaction records, investigate abuse, protect security, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods vary by information type and purpose. Backups may persist for a limited period after information is removed from active systems.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent. These rights may be subject to identity checks, exceptions and legal limitations.

You can also choose what profile and social content to publish, use available blocking controls, and manage device notification permissions. Some information is necessary to operate your account and core features.

11. Account Deletion and Data Deletion Requests

You may request account deletion by using an account-deletion option if it is available in the app, or by emailing the 2Chat support contact below from the email address associated with your account. Include your username and clearly state that you are requesting account deletion. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your account.

We will process a valid request in accordance with applicable law. Deletion may not be immediate and some information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, payment/accounting records, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution or enforcement of our Terms. Content already copied or saved by other users may not be recoverable or removable by 2Chat.

12. Children and Teen Users

2Chat registration currently requires users to be at least 13, but a higher age or parental-consent requirement may apply under the laws where a user lives. The Service is not intended for anyone who is below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can review the request.

Parents or legal guardians who believe a minor's account or information requires review may contact us. We may need to verify the request and the relationship to the minor before taking action.

13. Cookies, Local Storage and Similar Technologies

The website or app may use cookies, local storage, session tokens and similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, preferences and core functionality. Blocking or clearing these technologies may sign you out or prevent some features from working properly.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when our features, providers or legal obligations change. We will revise the date at the top and provide additional notice where required. Please review this page periodically.
