Request deletion of your 2Chat account

You can request deletion from inside the 2Chat app or through the account website. The account owner must sign in and confirm the request with their password.

From the 2Chat app

  1. Open 2Chat and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Go to Account & Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account and confirm your password.

From the web

Sign in with the account you want to delete, then follow the same Profile → Account & Settings → Delete Account steps.

What happens after you request deletion?

Your account is restricted immediately. A 30-day grace period starts when you submit the request. You can sign in and cancel the request during this period. If you do not cancel it, the deletion worker permanently removes your profile, posts, Shorts, statuses, messages, social relationships and associated media where the media is owned by 2Chat's Cloudinary account.

Some financial transaction, withdrawal, fraud-prevention and safety records may be retained where required for accounting, legal or security purposes. A request may be blocked while a creator cash balance or an active withdrawal remains unresolved.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account to submit a deletion request, use the contact details published in the 2Chat Privacy Policy.
